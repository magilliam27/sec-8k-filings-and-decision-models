# E7: S&P 500 members' 10-K risk factors and Item 1A sections

Data lane, issue magilliam27/MCI-GRU#309, code on PR magilliam27/MCI-GRU#300 (commit 1f868f3). Built 2026-10-16.

Every 10-K (and 10-KT) a company filed while it was in the S&P 500, accepted
2013-01-01 to 2026-10-15, plus the one report just before it joined, so every
member report has its pair partner. Two releases, the same code:

| release | companies | reports | member reports | risks published | member reports with risks left out | member reports with a section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1: still filing under a ticker in SEC's current list | 593 | 6,680 | 6,115 | 200,446 | 438 (7.2%) | 5,963 (97.5%) |
| r2: acquired, delisted or renamed | 159 | 980 | 828 | 27,478 | 44 (5.3%) | 816 (98.6%) |
| both | 752 | 7,660 | 6,943 | 227,924 | 482 (6.9%) | 6,779 (97.6%) |

Manifests: `filings/data/e7_member_10k_risk_factors.r1.json` and `.r2.json`
(paths inside are relative to `e7_sp500_10k/r1/` and `e7_sp500_10k/r2/`).

## Files

Each of `r1/` and `r2/`:

- `member_10k_index.csv`: one row per report: company key, CIK, accession,
  form, acceptance time (UTC), period of report, `role` (`member`, or `prior`
  for the report just before the company joined), `prior_accession` (the
  company's previous report by acceptance time, which is the pair partner; not by
  fiscal year, so a changed year end keeps both reports), the filer's SIC code,
  the layout the risks were read with, `risks_published`, `section_published`.
- `member_risk_factors.jsonl`: one risk per line (category, heading, body), only
  for reports whose split into risks passed all seven checks.
- `member_item_1a.jsonl`: the whole Item 1A per line, paragraphs joined by blank
  lines, with `section_words` and `risks_passed`. Every report whose Item 1A
  holds at least 400 words is here, whether or not its risks split cleanly: use
  this for whole-section measures such as Lazy Prices similarity.
- `member_extraction_report.csv`, `member_extraction_checks.csv`: per report
  counts, and every check finding.
- `member_left_out.csv`: every report whose risks are left out, with its year,
  SIC code, whether its section is still published, and the reason.
- `member_10k.meta.json`: provenance and counts, including left-out shares by
  year and by SIC division.

`membership/`:

- `sp500_ticker_runs.csv`: the mapping table, one row per ticker run in the
  index. `ciks` is the CIK chosen; `method` says how it was found (`wiki_cik`:
  Wikipedia's CIK column; `sec_ticker`: SEC's ticker file; `name_lookup`:
  EDGAR's company-name list; `manual`: `manual_names.csv`; `name_prefix`: name
  start only, used when nothing else confirmed). Every chosen CIK is confirmed
  by name: SEC's name for that CIK on each 10-K's filing date (from
  `formerNames`) matches the run's name (`sec_names`). `reports_counted` is how
  many reports confirmed it; `handed_over` lists runs split between two CIKs (Chubb Corp then
  ACE, 21st Century Fox then Fox Corp, and five more); `candidates` lists
  every CIK tried.
- `sp500_companies.csv`: one row per company, its CIKs in order (a new
  holding company continues its predecessor, e.g. Disney 2019), tickers,
  membership spells and release.
- `sp500_members.meta.json`: sources and counts for the two tables.
- `manual_names.csv`: 68 hand-set rows (name and CIK) for runs no source
  named well, each confirmed by name like any other row.
- `spot_check_result.csv`: 20 change dates checked against S&P Dow Jones
  press releases: 19 confirmed. The member lists drop a removal about a day
  early around spin-offs (XRAY, VNO, ESRX); BKR/BHI was not found.
- `wiki_vs_fja.csv`: where Wikipedia's changes table and the member lists differ.
- `inputs/`: the Wikipedia pages, SEC ticker file and EDGAR name list the
  table was built from.
  The member lists are `filings/data/e7_prep/sp500_historical_components_fja05680_2026-08-18.csv`
  (github.com/fja05680/sp500), topped up from Wikipedia's changes after 2026-08-18.

## How the risks were read

The parser and its six original checks are the E5/E9 ones (magilliam27/MCI-GRU#297, magilliam27/MCI-GRU#304), plus,
for magilliam27/MCI-GRU#309:

- **Layout fallback.** A company outside the hand-checked registry is read
  with the first of `blocks`, `run_in`, `sentences` whose reading passes every
  check. r1 used 6,227 blocks, 331 run_in and 122 sentences.
- **A seventh check, `section_text_not_in_risks`.** A reading fails when its
  risks hold under 70% of the section's words and 300 or more are missing. About
  one report in fifty had passed every other check while losing most of its text
  (Marriott fiscal 2018 kept 441 of 9,335 words). In these releases it flags
  54 r1 and 11 r2 reports that no layout reads in full.
- **Title rules.** The section's title repeated as a page header ("Item 1A.
  Risk Factors (Continued)") is not a risk; more forward-looking and summary
  titles open introduction; category titles may run to 400 characters. A
  title ending in a full stop is still a risk (CVS).

Alphabet's, E5's and E9's published risk files are reproduced byte for byte by
this code. r1 was run twice from the cache and gave identical files.

## Known limits

- Flagged reports are concentrated: of 94 r1 companies with a flagged report,
  18 have all their reports flagged (FedEx's and NOV's text under no heading
  among them). Their sections are still published.
- A 10-K/A is not read; the original 10-K stands.
- Membership dates come from the member lists; S&P announces changes days ahead,
  and a report filed on a change day could fall either side.
